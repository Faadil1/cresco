# CRESCO — Company thesis (draft)

Mission: enable people to delegate financial actions without surrendering control.

CRESCO Family remains the first product. Solana is an initial enforcement rail, not the boundary of the company or its market.

Possible future customers include families, organizations and developers building AI agents. These are hypotheses, not launched products or validated demand.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Preserve the complete Family-first journey, including refusal, revocation and recovery.
- Capital-path enforcement is authoritative; an interface or agent cannot override it.
- Consent must eventually bind onchain to the exact action, destination, amount, asset and nonce. This is not yet proven for historical ALLOW_ONCE.
- Distinguish simulated funds from real funds and label evidence honestly.
- Do not imply banking, custody, fiat integrations or regulatory compliance without validation.
- Do not alter the historical Colosseum submission.
- Do not build four products at once: prove Family, then test adjacent demand and technical reuse.

## Company validation

1. Complete Family onchain negative-path and recovery tests.
2. Conduct interviews with prospective family users and document unmet needs.
3. Compare existing family-finance, policy-wallet and agent-spend alternatives.
4. Establish a measurable willingness-to-pay signal.
5. Validate jurisdiction-specific regulatory and custody boundaries before real-money expansion.
6. Consider an external integration only when it has a specific user and demonstrable need.

## Funding principle

Solana ecosystem grants, investors, partners and customer revenue are distinct possible sources of support. No one funder defines the company's product or guarantees its viability.

Status: strategic working hypothesis; not product proof, investor claims or a replacement for the canonical PRD.
