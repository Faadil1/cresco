# Cresco - Final Integration Contract

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: CANONICAL FOR FINAL SUBMISSION INTEGRATION

## Core rule

The final Cresco build is the coordinated union of three mandatory sources:

1. Benita baseline: the complete product structure, navigation, responsive behavior, interactions and visual craft already implemented in cresco-lac / apps/web.
2. Winning visual direction: the selected visual-lab language, plus only the strongest compatible mechanisms from the other directions.
3. Post-baseline product/backend deltas: every semantic improvement, capability and proof added after Benita's baseline.

No source may silently overwrite another. The final build is a merge, not a reset.

## Non-regression rule

Nothing useful already present in Benita's product may disappear simply because the visual direction changes.

A removal is allowed only when it is an explicit product decision and its replacement preserves or improves the same user job. Every removal or replacement must be recorded in the final coverage matrix.

## Post-baseline deltas that must be integrated

Authority model:
- Key vN is a visible standing authority object.
- Inside means independent action with no guardian approval ceremony.
- Boundary means visible refusal with human reason and next actions.
- Guardian choices are Not this time / Allow once / Widen the Key.
- Allow once moves the exception, not the standing boundary.
- Widen creates a new standing Key version.
- Learning, XP, P&L and AI never widen authority automatically.

Exact-action proof:
- Canonical Devnet proof run: 36150024852.
- Guardian approves exact $12 request.
- Materially changed $11 action -> REFUSE / AllowanceActionMismatch.
- Approved $12 action -> ALLOW.
- Standing Key remains v7 -> v7.
- Permission becomes USED.
- Replay -> REFUSE / AllowanceAlreadyUsed.

Judge-safe line: One request. One exact approved amount. One successful use. The standing Key does not move.

Learning and representation:
- Contextual learning appears when useful, especially near boundaries.
- Learning is source-backed.
- Practice stays visually and semantically separate from Money.
- AAPL remains the proven Money lane.
- Other discovered markets remain Learn / Practice unless separately proven.
- Tessera / PreStocks belong to representation understanding, not automatic Money eligibility.
- Company != representation != eligibility != Key authority.

Proof hierarchy:
- Human mechanism first.
- Technical proof second.
- Solana / Pyth / nonce / signatures do not dominate the opening experience.
- Pyth may stop or restrict; it never grants authority.

History and privacy:
- Key history emphasizes boundary events, guardian decisions, one-time use and version changes.
- Parent experience must not become a surveillance feed of every in-bounds action.

## Winning-direction rule

The winning visual direction controls composition, metaphor, typography, motion grammar, hierarchy, surface treatment and emotional tone. It does not get to delete product depth.

Mechanisms from non-winning directions may be reused only when they solve a specific product problem better and still feel coherent in the winning system.

## Final merge gates

1. Benita coverage gate: every useful existing route/capability is preserved or intentionally replaced.
2. Product delta gate: every post-baseline semantic improvement is represented.
3. Backend truth gate: UI claims match deployed proof.
4. Visual coherence gate: winning grammar is applied across the whole product, not just hero screens.
5. Responsive gate: mobile and desktop preserve the authority loop.
6. Accessibility gate: focus, keyboard and reduced-motion behavior remain sound.
7. Judge-demo gate: the canonical story completes end-to-end without verbal rescue.
8. No-fake-success gate: pending, stale, unavailable and refusal states remain honest.

## Required coverage matrix

Before final promotion, maintain one matrix with columns:

Benita baseline | Winning direction treatment | Post-baseline delta | Backend truth | Mobile | Accessibility | Demo relevance | Final status

No row may remain unreviewed.

## Final principle

Preserve what already works. Add what became stronger. Redesign the whole system coherently. Remove nothing by accident.

Cresco becomes unique through coordination: consumer clarity, family authority, visual language, contextual learning, market truth and Solana enforcement all behaving as one product rather than as disconnected features.
