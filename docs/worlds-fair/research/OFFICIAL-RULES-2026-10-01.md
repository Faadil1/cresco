## Judging criteria

1. Functionality
2. Potential Impact
3. Novelty
4. UX
5. Open-source / composability
6. Business Plan / team execution

## Submission constraints relevant to CRESCO

- Each entrant may only be on one team.
- A team may only submit one project.
- Team members must register and the team leader submits before the deadline.
- Submitted content must be in English.
- The Solana track awards the best products that integrate with the Solana blockchain.
