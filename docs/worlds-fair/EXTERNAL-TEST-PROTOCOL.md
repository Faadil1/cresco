# External test: CRESCO in 5 to 10 minutes

> Superseded for round 1 by `USER-TEST-KIT-2026-10-12.md` (tracks, measures, Google Form script). The owner checklist below still applies.

For Benita, friends and unknown testers. **No Phantom, no private key, no funds.** Every tester creates their own workspace, so no role token is ever shared publicly.

## Before sending the link (owner)

- Use only a URL you verified in your own browser after the latest deploy (the home page, `/`). Never send a workspace link: those carry role tokens.
- Check the guardian's Devnet SOL and devUSDC. Each tester workspace spends some.
- Capacity is 25 workspaces in total by default. For a larger wave, set the Worker variable `WORLD_FAIR_MAX_WORKSPACES` (1 to 200) and redeploy the API Worker, after checking that the lab funds cover it.

## What the tester does (copy into the form)

1. Open the CRESCO link. In one sentence, what do you think CRESCO does?
2. Choose **Family** (or Delegated capital if you work with trading or AI agents) and click Enter.
3. Keep the default names and limit. Click **Create workspace**, then "Continue to the desk". You get the three people's links; you do not need to share them.
4. Click **Bootstrap mandate** and wait for READY (up to a minute).
5. In the Teen view, invest **$0.30**. What happened?
6. Invest **$0.75**. What happened, and why?
7. Switch to the Parent view. Approve the request. What changed, and what did not change?
8. Open **History** and click one receipt link. Did the receipt help you trust what happened?
9. In the Parent view, click **Pause**, go back to the Teen view and try $0.30 again.

## Questionnaire

Scales are 1 to 5 unless noted.
1. Before trying it, how clear was what CRESCO is for?
2. Did you complete steps 3 to 7 without help? (yes / with help / no). Where did you get stuck?
3. After step 6, could you explain why the $0.75 needed approval?
4. After step 7, did you understand that the limit stayed the same?
5. Was anything confusing or broken? (free text, plus a screenshot if possible)
6. Would this solve a real problem for you or someone you know? Who?
7. Family or delegated capital: which felt more relevant, and why?
8. What would you need before using it with real money?
9. One thing to change first.
10. Time spent (minutes).

## How to classify the answers

- This is **usability and comprehension evidence**. It is not customer validation and not willingness to pay.
- Eric Lovold's quote stays an **expert testimony** (with his permission), never a customer.
