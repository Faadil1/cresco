# Cresco Family — clarify the two roles at first glance
Date: 2026-10-29
Status: source-only draft, no Family deployment.

## User problem

The earlier Family landing page led with “Room to learn. Rules you control,” but emphasized setup links and wallet recovery before showing a teen making a choice. The first-screen positioning could be misunderstood as an infrastructure product rather than a family-led learning experience.

## Changes

- Headline now explicitly identifies the **young person acting** and the **parent defining boundaries**.
- A side-by-side experience preview labels Teen and Parent viewpoints within the first screen. The teen sees **imaginary credits**, their own choice and a proposed question. The parent sees what they can consider, approve or decline.
- The main call-to-action goes to the existing interactive same-tab practice story; a secondary link leads to the Parent Trust Center, with separate technical desk access retained below.
- Text explicitly states **no real account, no custody, no brokerage, no stablecoin earnings, no stock order, no actual parent/teen relationship or financial permission** in this educational preview.
- Tests assert role visibility, correct no-money disclosures and non-financial call-to-action targets.

## Truth / legal / release boundaries

The hero is a *static example*, not a synchronized family dashboard. The practice demo is in temporary client state. No actual youth account, guardian verified identity or real assets are available in the independent Family release. Real-world user research is not yet completed.

Do not merge the Family branch into the historical Colosseum main. Do not deploy `cresco` or `keys-api-stocklana`; new Family Workers, state, secrets and a separate release sign-off are still required.

## Immediate test

Show the first screen to 5–8 consenting adults and ask without coaching:
1. Who starts decisions?
2. What does the parent control?
3. Are the numbers real money or fictional?
4. What does “approve” do in the practice story?
5. What risk or disclosure is unclear?

Document task observations. Do not collect identifiable children's information. An actual minor study requires a separate consent/privacy/safeguarding protocol.
