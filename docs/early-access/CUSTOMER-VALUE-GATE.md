# Customer Value Gate — CRESCO project-local decision contract

**Status:** DRAFT / additive to Product Reality v1.5 and existing QUALIFY -> DECIDE -> DESIGN -> DELIVER -> AUDIT -> EXPAND lifecycle. **No new global stage, score, agent, or independent authority.** Any decision to invite more users, ship code, merge, deploy, spend or use Mainnet remains controlled by existing human and technical release gates.

## What is this gate for?

The question is not "does a Devnet demo work?" but "is the target user's recurring job real, did a safe version help them, and is further exposure justified?" Scope the answer to the **specific buyer cohort, product version, actual task and evidence time window**. Do not aggregate Parent/Family and Capital/Agent segments into one adoption statistic.

## Decision rubric — use evidence, not invented minimums

### Q1: Is the problem real for qualified buyers?
- **Required:** recent episode with trigger, actual alternative, cost/consequence if any, and at least one counterexample or explicit unknown.
- **Insufficient:** marketing interest, social praise, hackathon win, expert endorsement, a hypothetical buyer persona.

### Q2: Did Cresco produce first value?
- **Required:** qualified participant attempted an authentic safe task, with exact tested version, outcome, assistance, mistakes, and possible alternative explanation.
- **Insufficient:** creating an account, opening the page, wallet connection, local CI green, seeded scenario or founder-assisted walkthrough reported as unassisted.

### Q3: Is it safe and understandable?
- **Required:** no unmitigated severe trust/authority issue; participants understand their standing limit does not change because of one exception; technical/runtime/privacy gates permit the tested path.
- **Immediate PAUSE trigger:** unauthorized action, misleading claim of securities or execution, exposed token/secrets/minor data, unchecked loss/risk, inability to reconcile a financial outcome or a serious uncontained incident.
- **Absence of incidents in a few sessions is not proof of broad safety.**

### Q4: Is the value repeatable and economically meaningful?
- **Required for expansion claim:** a subsequent real opportunity to perform the job and observed voluntary re-engagement without excessive founder help, plus an identifiable likely buyer and a genuine, separately evidenced commitment when pricing claims are made.
- **Insufficient:** "I'd use it", "I'd pay", return to a mandatory follow-up demo, founder-requested artificial task.

### Q5: Are we ready to widen?
- **Required:** evidence review with denominators, selection bias, missing data, risk log, alternative explanations and explicit owner/date/next check. New users are not automatically approved because metrics looked good.
- **If unclear:** restrict access, continue observation, reframe market or pause.

## Possible outputs

- **ADVANCE_BOUNDED:** specific user cohort and build may advance to the next approved research round; not a release authorization.
- **REPEAT_TEST:** same-job observation and controlled fix/retest before conclusion.
- **REFRAME:** adjust target job or separate cohort (e.g., delegated capital instead of Family) after recording counterevidence.
- **PAUSE:** safety, privacy, consent or execution uncertainty.
- **STOP_HYPOTHESIS:** documented mismatch between real job and proposed solution.

## Minimum Decision Receipt

`gate_id | cohort | user_job | version | participant_selection | observed_episode_sources | attempt_count | unassisted_value_count | recurring_opportunities | observed_returns | severe_incidents | counterexamples | competing_explanations | decision | owner | date | next_trigger | release_authorization=NONE`

No score can substitute for the underlying records. Five of eight qualified interviews is not eight customers. A successful user task is not a legal permission to provide financial services.

The [existing user-test kit](../worlds-fair/USER-TEST-KIT-2026-10-12.md) continues to govern its A/B/C tasks. Product Reality and the Reality Ledger remain the sources for "LIVE" claims. This gate can only **block or recommend further bounded research**; protected decisions remain with the existing human owner.
