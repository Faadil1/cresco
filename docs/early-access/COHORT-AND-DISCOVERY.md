# CRESCO — qualified parent discovery and opt-in cohort

**Current status:** NOT RECRUITED. Targets below are planning caps, not current participants, response rates or proof.

## Recruit for the job, not general enthusiasm

**Family priority:** begin with approximately 5–8 consenting **adults** who have recently needed to set, explain or override a young person's spending permissions. Among them, aim for a mix of existing-method types and at least two who do **not** know the founder or the hackathon. Do not call these numbers representative of a population. At this stage an adult may role-play the delegate; no minor participation is necessary to find obvious workflow failures.

**Delegated capital:** separate prospective allocator/risk owner/agent builder or trading operator cohort. Do not substitute an AI-agent enthusiast for a parent, or count the same person twice as independent demand evidence.

**Expert channel:** ecosystem members, security reviewers, advisers. Useful for technical risks and introductions; label EXPERT_REVIEW rather than CUSTOMER_EVIDENCE.

## Qualification and counterexamples

Ask each adult before scheduling (minimum necessary; no account numbers, family financial details or child's identifying data):

1. What responsibility do you currently have for setting or approving another person's spending decisions?
2. About when did you last have to approve or refuse a specific spending request? May we discuss the sequence in general terms, without private amounts or personal identifiers?
3. Which method did you use: conversation, cash, bank/prepaid card, text, another tool, or none?
4. How often does this type of situation recur? If it does not recur, that is valuable negative evidence.
5. Are you comfortable with a short research interview or a strictly demo-token product session? Would you prefer not to be recorded?
6. Are you willing to be contacted **once** about an optional follow-up? No consent means no follow-up.

A parent whose current solution works flawlessly is a valuable **counterexample**, not an exclusion from analysis. Track who declined and who was silent only in private aggregate operational counts.

## Interview guide — 15–25 minutes

**Neutral opening:** "We're researching how families actually handle spending independence and exceptions. We want to understand what happened in real situations; this isn't a sales call."

- "Tell me about the **last time** you needed to set a limit or approve/refuse an expense. What triggered it?"
- "What did the young person do first? What did you do next? Which tools or messages were involved?"
- "What was difficult, if anything? What did it cost in time, friction or attention?"
- "When did the current method work well? What made it work?"
- "When a request was exceptional, did you make a one-time exception or change the standing rule? What did you worry about?"
- "What would need to remain true for you to trust any alternative?"
- "Did a similar need arise again? What did you do the second time?"
- "What would you never want a product to decide without you?"

Only **after** collecting the existing workflow may the moderator show the CRESCO concept and test whether it maps to that job. Do not lead with "Would you use/buy our app?" or ask for bank balances, addresses or ages/identities of minors.

## Access Contract (explain before any session)

- **What exists:** unfinished CRESCO research/demo, some functionality on Solana Devnet with demo tokens, no live banking/brokerage/mainnet/real minor investments.
- **What a person may do:** conversation; optionally use a verified safe build in a bounded, supervised role-play task.
- **What is expected:** honest feedback and optional retest; no obligation to promote, invest or pay.
- **Support:** name the session moderator and safe contact route privately; record assistance.
- **Privacy:** pseudonymous ID; no private family details, raw financial data, wallet keys, screen share of private accounts, recordings without separate affirmative consent.
- **Leaving:** stop immediately, decline questions or withdraw future contact without penalty. Explain any retention/deletion limitations of recorded data before collecting it.
- **Young people:** initial cohort is adult-only; direct research involving minors requires separately approved safeguarding, privacy, guardian consent and any legally necessary assent. No real securities, wallets, or money exposure.

## Recruitment and follow-up boundaries

Do not auto-contact, scrape personal profiles, DM uninvited people en masse, share Telegram chat screenshots or infer consent from joining a group. First seek opt-in invitations or introductions with permission. If an intermediary provides an introduction, ask the candidate whether to participate; do not treat a warm intro as a participant.

Stage transitions: identified -> invited -> opted_in -> enabled -> attempted -> first_value -> returned_on_real_need. Never merge "invited" and "first value".

## When discovery can move to task testing

- At least one verifiable (participant-reported) recent job episode from a qualified adult, with the **existing method** and a counterexample or documented unknown.
- Participant understands the demo/token and research limits.
- A version-verified, safe, funded-if-needed environment and moderator are ready.
- If these are absent: mark **BLOCKED** or continue discovery, not "early-access launched".
