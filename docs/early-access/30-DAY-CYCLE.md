## What to watch (with denominators)

- `invited / consented / qualified / enabled / attempted / unaided-first-value / assisted-first-value / failed-or-unknown`
- Task-level time, hints, explanation of unchanged limit and hard-stop comprehension.
- Real episode frequency **as reported**, observed repeat-need opportunity and voluntary return when it happens.
- Support minutes, inability to bootstrap, lab capacity and experience of refused/UNKNOWN operations.
- Negative cases and cohort counterexamples **remain in the record** when later resolved.
- Real economic action: buyer intro, separate call with budget owner, paid trial intent tied to an actual purchasing process, paid contract. Never label a hypothetical willingness-to-pay response as revenue.

The research product is a **documented decision from genuine people and tasks**, not a prettier demo or new count of GitHub artifacts.

## Explicit stop/rollback

- Immediate PAUSE for privacy, authorization or trust-boundary concern.
- If public build/version cannot be bound, stick to safe interviews/manual method observation; no live-product invitation.
- If the best finding is that current solutions suffice, record it as an outcome, not a recruitment failure.
- If all enthusiasm comes only from friendly/technical ecosystem members, keep Family demand **UNKNOWN**.
- No merge, no Prod/Devnet deploy, no external outreach automation, no modification to historical Colosseum submission from this documentation-only PR.
